> Describe your problem, what makes it unique, and why it excites you. Please summarize the research you've conducted to determine that either the problem has not been solved or the available solutions are insufficient.
As a former college and now professional pitcher, I've received very detailed scouting reports each week about the opposing teams hitters. While these documents and scouting reports contain valueable information its often difficult to digest and hard to reconcile with my own pitching strengths. For my final project I want to develop an AI scouting assistant that takes in both my personal pitching profile and infomation and the opposing hitter scouting reports and have it create a concise personalized game plan, that I can remember during the game. For example, it might recommend attacking a patient leadoff hitter with fastballs early in the count or throwing breaking balls early against a strong fastball hitter. My preliminary research has shown that platforms like TruMedia, Opponent Scout AI, and Pitch MetRx already provide scouting analysis, and in some cases, personalized recommendations. However, I believe there is still an opportunity to improve how this information is translated into a practical game plan. My goal is to build an AI assistant that works directly with the scouting reports pitchers already receive, incorporates their individual strengths, and condenses the information into a few actionable points that are easy to remember during a game. I don't want to replace a pitcher's instincts or coaches' advice, but rather make existing information easier to understand and apply.

> Explain why the problem is complex enough that you could not solve it without the help of AI.
This is a difficult problem, scouting reports might conflict with one another. The program would need to be able to identify what's important information and what isn't. This kind of program is not something that I would feasably be able to solve on my own. I also want to give the program a very nice GUI interface and I think AI would particulary help me with that.

> List the expertise that you bring to this problem space and how it helps tame the problem's complexity.
As I mentioned before, I played baseball at Harvard and now I play professional baseball for the Chicago White Sox. My expertise and knowellege of the game of baseball will allow me to understand when the program is giving me good or bad reports. This will certainly bring down the problem's complexity because I will have plenty of my own experince with scouting and also my own personal scouting reports from the past seasons that I compare with. 

> Recall your course goals. State how this project advances these goals and takes you beyond your comfort zone.
One of my main goals for this course is to become more comfortable using AI to solve complex, real-world problems rather than simply using it to generate answers or code. This project would allow me to explore how AI can interpret large amounts of information, identify important patterns, and turn those findings into useful recommendations. It would also push me beyond my comfort zone because I would need to figure out how to build an AI agent that can process scouting reports, incorporate my personal pitching strengths, and produce accurate, personalized game plans. While I have experience analyzing baseball scouting reports as a pitcher, I have never built a system that can perform this kind of analysis automatically. I think this project would help me develop my skills in AI integration, prompt engineering, and evaluating the reliability of AI-generated recommendations.
